## Multiple-choice questions

1. An image-only PDF with no useful text should:

   - A. Count as successful empty ingestion
   - B. Invent a summary
   - C. Reuse a previous document’s text
   - D. Report needs-review/OCR/unsupported under the declared policy

2. TXT in the baseline can be read using:

   - A. Explicit UTF-8 and line locators
   - B. A mandatory image model
   - C. Vector distance
   - D. Random binary decode

## Conceptual questions

1. How does parsing differ from chunking? Give a concrete counterexample and the expected safe behavior.
2. Why retain structure when possible? Explain the invariant and how a later test would detect its violation.

## Practical assignment

Complete [the module lab](../labs/lab-08.md); submit its artifact plus an independent failure fixture. Execution belongs to the later implementation pass.

## Debugging scenarios

Attempt both lecture debugging questions. For each, identify the earliest failing stage, the observed evidence, the safe response, and a regression test. Do not assume a model/framework/database change is the first fix.

## Review criteria

- Both multiple-choice answers correct and justified.
- The module-specific contract and counterexample are concrete.
- Lab and failure evidence cover every acceptance criterion.
- No unrun project test, quality score, or completion is claimed.
