## Multiple-choice questions

   - A. 0
   - B. Their dimension
   - C. 1
   - D. Undefined

2. Which statement is valid for normalized vectors?

   - A. Dot product equals cosine
   - B. Euclidean distance sorts descending
   - C. Zero vectors normalize safely
   - D. Scores are probabilities

2. Prove the ranking relationship between cosine, dot product, and Euclidean distance under unit normalization; state the invalid-vector cases.
